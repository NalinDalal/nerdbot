## Excluded source: OpenBot

`~/OpenBot` is the data directory of an installed third-party Electron
application (`app.openbot.desktop`), not a source repository. No code, data,
configuration or other material from it is incorporated into Nerdbot, and it is
excluded from the adoption inventory. The identifiers `onOpenBot` / `openBot`
appearing in the dashboard are unrelated React prop and function names.
